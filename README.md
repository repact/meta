# Privacy Policy – Monkey Plaza

**Last updated: October 25, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
Monkey Plaza VR respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you play our game.

## 2. Information We Collect
Monkey Plaza VR does not intentionally collect personal information such as your real name, home address, or phone number.

Some information may be collected automatically by the platform, such as basic gameplay data, device information, crash reports, and technical information needed to keep the game working.

## 3. Voice Chat and Multiplayer
If the game includes multiplayer or voice chat, your voice and in-game actions may be transmitted to other players so you can communicate and play together.

Do not share personal information with other players.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The game may use third-party services, such as Meta Quest platform services, to provide multiplayer features, analytics, or technical support. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Children's Privacy
We do not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been collected through the game, please contact us.

## 6. Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the game. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its features change. Any updates will be published on this page.
